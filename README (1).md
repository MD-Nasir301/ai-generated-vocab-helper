# 📖 VocabCraft — Bilingual Vocabulary Builder (English ↔ Bengali)
> **A modern, interactive web tool to enrich English and Bengali vocabulary effortlessly.**

**VocabCraft** is a sleek web application designed for language learners. While reading or pasting any article or text, simply dragging or selecting text with your mouse instantly fetches and stores its meaning, part of speech, and example sentences.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 🔄 Bilingual Translation Mode
- **English ➔ Bengali Mode:** Paste English text and select any English word to get its Bengali meaning instantly.
- **Bengali ➔ English Mode:** Paste Bengali text and select any Bengali word to get its English meaning instantly.

### 2. ⚡ Selection Settings (Auto-Add vs. Ask First)
- Easily toggle your preferred selection behavior from the top navigation bar:
  - **⚡ Auto-Add Mode:** Instantly adds the selected word and meaning to your list as soon as you highlight it.
  - **❓ Ask First Mode:** Prompts a popup after selection; the word is only added when you explicitly click the "Add Word" button.

### 3. 🏷️ `P` Button — Part of Speech Toggle
- Click the **`P`** button above the word box to show or hide grammatical roles (e.g., `noun`, `verb`, `adjective`).
- **Export Control:** If enabled (`P` is active), exported text or images will include the part of speech; if disabled, it will be excluded.

### 4. 💡 `S` Button — Example Sentence Toggle
- Click the **`S`** button above the word box to display or hide **English example sentences and their Bengali translations** for each word.
- **Export Control:** If enabled (`S` is active), sentences and translations are included in the export; otherwise, only the word and meaning are exported.

### 5. 🎨 Smooth Bengali Typography (Hind Siliguri)
- Uses Google Fonts' elegant **`Hind Siliguri`** typography for Bengali text instead of standard system fonts, ensuring smooth, clear reading of conjuncts and sentences.

### 6. 📄 Clean Document-Style Export (TXT & Image)
- Two dedicated export buttons at the bottom: **Download TXT** and **Download Image**.
